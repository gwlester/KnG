Getting started

Connecting to your church's Server

Start here, for everyone · about 1:55

Find the Server, request enrollment, and get approved. The same steps for every app.

Transcript

This is the video everyone needs first. In this video: connecting to your church's Server.

Everything in Virtual Church Musician lives on one computer at your church: the Church Music Server. Every app talks to it over your church's network. All you need is to be on the same Wi-Fi.

When you open any app, it looks for the Server by itself. This is called Discover. You'll see a progress bar while it searches.

If your church has more than one Server, for example on two campuses, the app asks which to use. It remembers your choice.

The very first time, a brand-new Server has no administrator yet. If you see a message asking you to finish setup from the Admin app, that's what it means. The administrator does that step; it's covered in the administrator videos.

Everyone else sees Enroll This Device. Type your username, add a label such as 'Lobby tablet' or 'Office PC' so the administrator can tell devices apart, and choose Request Enrollment.

A Waiting for Approval window appears. Someone with the Administration Console has to approve you. You can leave this window open, or choose Check Now once you know it's done.

Once you're approved, you'll be asked to choose a password and agree to the license.

Choose a password, agree to the license, and you're in.

If the app can't find the Server: check you're on the same Wi-Fi, try Discover a second time, and if it still can't, the Server may be off. Ask your technical contact.

That's connecting to your church's Server, and everyone needs this one. Next, choose the videos for your job on the Videos page: administrators, service planners and whoever presses Play each have their own.
