    A quick tour: how the VCM Server, the apps and your sound system fit together, and what a service looks like from planning to Play.

    Transcript

    This is a quick tour of Virtual Church Musician, the music system for your church's worship. In this video: how the pieces fit together.

    Virtual Church Musician puts the music for your worship service on one computer at your church, the VCM Server. The apps on your church's other computers and phones all talk to it over your network.

    Different people use different apps. Someone designs the templates. Someone plans each week's service. Someone presses Play. And an administrator looks after the Server and approves who can connect.

    The service template designer uses VCM Templates. A template is a reusable starting point: the order of a service, and if you like, the hymns and chants.

    Each week, the service planner opens VCM Services, starts from a template, picks a date, and fills in that week's hymns. Items in red still need one.

    When every item is fixed, the planner presses Publish. That saves the service on the Server, where anyone with VCM Runner can find it.

    On Sunday, whoever presses Play opens VCM Runner, loads today's service, and presses Play. The Server plays the music through your sound system.

    And the administrator uses VCM Administrator. Every new device asks to connect, and the administrator approves it, or rejects it.

    That's the tour. To get started, watch 'Connecting to your church's Server', and then choose the videos for your job on the Training page.

    This video shows how to install a VCM app on a Windows computer. Your screens may look a little different from these. The steps are the same. In this video: installing the apps on Windows.

    There are four apps for your computer: VCM Administrator, VCM Templates, VCM Services and VCM Runner. You install each one the same way, and you only need the ones for your job.

    Then follow the installer. Choose the button that continues, and again, until it finishes. You don't need to change any of the settings.

    When it's done, open the Start menu and type the name of the app, for example, V C M Administrator. Choose it from the list, and it opens.

    To install another app, repeat these steps with that app's file. You only do this once for each app on each computer.

    Connecting to your church's Server

    Start here, for everyone · about 1:55

    Transcript

    This is the video everyone needs first. In this video: connecting to your church's Server.

    Everything in Virtual Church Musician lives on one computer at your church: the VCM Server. Every app talks to it over your church's network. All you need is to be on the same Wi-Fi.

    When you open any app, it looks for the Server by itself. This is called Discover. You'll see a progress bar while it searches.

    If your church has more than one Server, for example on two campuses, the app asks which to use. It remembers your choice.

    The very first time, a brand-new Server has no administrator yet. If you see a message asking you to finish setup from VCM Administrator, that's what it means. The administrator does that step; it's covered in the administrator videos.

    Everyone else sees Enroll This Device. Type your username, add a label such as 'Lobby tablet' or 'Office PC' so the administrator can tell devices apart, and choose Request Enrollment.

    A Waiting for Approval window appears. Someone with VCM Administrator has to approve you. You can leave this window open, or choose Check Now once you know it's done.

    Once you're approved, you'll be asked to choose a password and agree to the license.

    Choose a password, agree to the license, and you're in.

    If the app can't find the Server: check you're on the same Wi-Fi, try Discover a second time, and if it still can't, the Server may be off. Ask your technical contact.

    That's connecting to your church's Server, and everyone needs this one. Next, choose the videos for your job on the Training page: administrators, service planners and whoever presses Play each have their own.

    Managing devices and users

    For administrators · about 1:25

    Transcript

    This video assumes your device is already connected. If it isn't, watch 'Connecting to your church's Server' first. In this video: managing devices and users.

    To block a device for a while, select it and choose Suspend. The device can't connect until you say so. Use this when you're not sure yet what happened.

    To start a new template from an existing one without changing the original, select it and choose Clone. That's ideal for a service that's almost, but not quite, the same.

    Every save creates a new version, and the older ones are kept. Rebase renumbers the newest one back to version 1 once you don't need the old ones. And on a computer, you can Export a template to a file, or Import one someone shared with you.
