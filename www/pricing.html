Pricing

Pay once. Keep it.

The apps your church uses day to day are free. You buy the VCM Server, and the VCM MIDI Player if your instrument is not next to the Server. Each is a one-time purchase, priced in US dollars.

VCM Server

$500 one time

Stores your hymnals, hymns, chants, templates, and services, and plays the music at the right moment.

VCM MIDI Player

$200 one time

Sits next to your organ or keyboard, receives MIDI files from the Server over your church network, and plays them.

Desktop and mobile apps

Free

VCM Templates, VCM Services, VCM Runner, VCM Administrator, and VCM Security for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android.

What is included

Updates are part of the price

  • Minor version upgrades and patches are included (for example 1.0 to 1.1, or 1.1.2 to 1.1.3) for as long as you hold a valid license.
  • A new major version (for example 1.x to 2.0) may require an additional purchase, as stated when it becomes available.
  • 30-day refund. You may request a refund within 30 days after purchase, provided you have stopped using the software and removed all copies.
