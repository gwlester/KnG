FindCache Privacy Policy

Effective: October 29, 2026

The short version: FindCache has no account, no ads and no analytics. The places you save stay on your phone. FindCache does not send them, your location or anything else about you to the developer.

Who we are

FindCache is developed by Gerald Lester, an individual. Contact: inquiries@kng-consulting.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Your saved places (name, coordinates, accuracy, notes), your find log, your sharing name, and your settings are stored in a database on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Your location is read by Android while a FindCache screen is open, to show distance and direction. It is not recorded except when you save a place, and it never leaves the phone. FindCache does not use location in the background.

What leaves your phone, and only when you ask

  • Bluetooth sharing. When you share places, the places you pick and your sharing name are sent over Bluetooth Low Energy to a nearby phone running FindCache that connects to you. While the Share screen is open your sharing name is visible to nearby devices. FindCache keeps a local history of who received places from you.
  • Export. Files you export (GPX or GeoJSON) go wherever you send them.
  • Maps. When you open a map, or the compass shows the street map or satellite imagery, FindCache downloads map tiles over HTTPS from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org, OpenStreetMap data) and the U.S. Geological Survey (basemap.nationalmap.gov). Those services see your IP address and which map tiles you request, which indicates the area you are viewing. They have their own privacy practices. Maps you downloaded for offline use need no connection. FindCache works without maps.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. There are no accounts, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.

Permissions

Location (to show distance and direction), Bluetooth and "Nearby devices" (to share with other phones), and network access (to download map tiles).

Children

FindCache is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.

Contact

Questions: inquiries@kng-consulting.com.
