## Product name

Virtual Church Musician (VCM)

## Category

Church Management Software (Capterra's closest existing category; VCM
covers the music/service-planning and live-playback slice of that
category, not donations, scheduling, or membership management)

## Tagline

Plan a church service, then run it live -- with MIDI or MP3 accompaniment
playing automatically, on cue.

## Description

Virtual Church Musician is a small suite of apps built specifically for
planning and running the music portion of a church service. Music and
worship staff build reusable templates, assemble a specific week's
service from hymns and chants, and hand it to a Runner app that plays the
service live -- sending stereo MP3 audio or MIDI directly to an
instrument (or through a dedicated MIDI Player) at the right cue, with no
one needing to operate playback by hand during the service itself. An
Administrator app and companion Security app manage which devices and
users are enrolled. Everything runs against a Server that stays fully
air-gapped on the church's own network -- nothing is sent back to the
vendor.

Desktop apps (Admin, Services, Templates, Runner) run on Windows, macOS,
and Linux. Mobile companions (Services, Templates, Runner, Security) run
on Android. The apps ship in English, Spanish, and German.

## Key features

- Reusable service templates, distinct from a specific week's build
- Live playback engine: stereo MP3 and direct MIDI output, cued
  automatically during the service
- Device/user enrollment and administration across a church's own network
- Fully air-gapped Server -- no data leaves the church's network by
  default
- Cross-platform desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux) and Android mobile apps
- Localized UI (English, Spanish, German)

## Similar tools

In the same general category: **HymnSoft**, **Christian Worship: Service
Builder** (with its **Playlist** add-on), and **MyMIDI Worship
Resources**. The clearest capability difference from Service Builder +
Playlist: Playlist plays back encoded audio recordings (MP3/M4A) instead
of MIDI, by its publisher's own stated design; VCM sends MIDI directly to
a real instrument during the live service.

## Pricing model

Freemium: Admin/Security, Templates, Services, and Runner client apps are
free. The Server and MIDI Player are commercially licensed per church --
contact for pricing (sales channel not yet finalized as of this writing).

## Target users

Music directors, worship leaders, and volunteer church AV/tech teams at
small to mid-size churches.
