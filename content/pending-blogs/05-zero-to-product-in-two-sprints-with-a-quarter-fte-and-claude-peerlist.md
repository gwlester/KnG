Shipped 10 applications in two sprints at roughly a quarter of one
person's working time: 4 desktop apps, 4 React Native apps, and the 2
servers behind them -- every desktop app and both servers cross-platform
for Windows, macOS, and Linux from the first release.

The build was Virtual Church Musician (church service planning + live
MIDI-driven playback). The part worth sharing isn't the AI pairing itself
-- it's what that freed the human quarter-FTE to focus on almost
exclusively: product decisions, not implementation. Once a feature's
shape was decided, the implementation, tests, and documentation got
drafted together as one unit and reviewed as one unit, which is most of
where the time actually came from.
