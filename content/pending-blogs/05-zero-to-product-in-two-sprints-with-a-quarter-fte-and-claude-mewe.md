Small-team/indie folks: wrote up how a real product (Virtual Church
Musician) -- 4 desktop apps, 4 React Native apps, and 2 servers, all
cross-platform for Windows/macOS/Linux -- went from nothing to shipped in

decisions, let the AI draft everything downstream of them, and never skip
the full test run just because time's tight.
