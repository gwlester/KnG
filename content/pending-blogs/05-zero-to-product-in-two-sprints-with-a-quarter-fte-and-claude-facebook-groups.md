We build software for churches (Virtual Church Musician -- service
building, templates, and live playback with MIDI to an instrument), and
just wrapped a stretch where one person, at about a quarter of their time,
took it from nothing to 10 real shipped apps in two sprints: four desktop
apps, four phone apps, and the two servers behind them, all running on
Windows, macOS, and Linux.

The only way that was possible was leaning hard on an AI pair for the
repetitive parts and spending almost all the actual human time on
deciding what a church needs the software to do -- not on typing it.
